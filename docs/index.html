Smart Glass Canary

An ESP32 that warns you when camera glasses are in Bluetooth range.

Your browser can't flash. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop. Flashing requires a secure (https) connection.

Chrome or Edge on desktop. Firefox, Safari and phones can't flash.

  1. Plug the ESP32 in with a USB data cable.
  2. Click Install.
  3. Enter your Wi-Fi when the page asks.

What it can't do. Meta glasses stop broadcasting once they're paired to a phone, so the canary catches them powering on or pairing but can go quiet during recording. A detection means glasses are nearby; silence doesn't mean nobody is recording.

Charge-only cables

Many USB cables carry power but no data. The board will light up and still not appear to your computer. If Install can't find a device, try a different cable first.

Wi-Fi and MQTT

Wi-Fi is set up over the USB connection, so there's no network to join. Credentials are stored on the device, not built into the firmware.

MQTT is optional. Without it the canary still blinks and beeps on its own. With it, it publishes to canary/glasses/*.
